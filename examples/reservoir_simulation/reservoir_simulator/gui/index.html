NVRS-CUDA

GPU-resident, fully implicit black-oil reservoir simulator

The model

Three-phase black oil with dissolved gas and vaporised oil. For each component \alpha \in \{o,w,g\} the mass balance over a control volume is

\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\Bigl(\phi \sum_{\beta} c_{\alpha\beta}\, b_\beta S_\beta\Bigr) + \nabla\cdot\sum_{\beta} c_{\alpha\beta}\, b_\beta \mathbf{u}_\beta = q_\alpha, \qquad \mathbf{u}_\beta = -\mathbf{K}\,\lambda_\beta\bigl(\nabla p_\beta - \rho_\beta g \nabla z\bigr),

with b_\beta = 1/B_\beta, \lambda_\beta = k_{r\beta}/\mu_\beta, c_{\alpha\alpha}=1, c_{go}=R_s and c_{og}=R_v. Two-point flux approximation on a Cartesian or corner-point grid gives, for the connection from i to j,

F_{\beta} = T_{ij}\,\lambda_\beta^{\mathrm{up}}\,\Delta\Phi_\beta, \qquad \Delta\Phi_\beta = (p_{\beta,j}-p_{\beta,i}) - \bar\rho_\beta\, g\,(z_j - z_i).

Each implicit step solves R(x)=0 by Newton, J(x^k)\,\delta x = -R(x^k), with b_s primaries per cell: (p,S_w) for oil-water and (p,S_w,x) for three phases, where x is S_g, R_s or R_v by phase state.

The linear solve

The pressure block is close to elliptic and the transport block close to hyperbolic, so constrained pressure residual treats them apart. With restriction \mathbf{R}_p and decoupling weights \mathbf{D},

M^{-1} = M_2^{-1}\bigl(I - J M_1^{-1}\bigr) + M_1^{-1}, \qquad M_1^{-1} = \mathbf{R}_p^{\mathsf T}\, \widetilde{A_p^{-1}}\, \mathbf{R}_p \mathbf{D}, \qquad A_p = \mathbf{R}_p \mathbf{D} J \mathbf{R}_p^{\mathsf T},

where \widetilde{A_p^{-1}} is one multigrid V-cycle from AmgX, BoomerAMG or Ginkgo and M_2 is a block-Jacobi sweep. Flexible GMRES accepts a preconditioner that changes between applications.

Precision

The state, the residual and the Krylov recurrences stay in FP64, so the limit of the iteration does not depend on the precision of the preconditioner. Reduced precision changes only the rate, and single precision is admissible when

\kappa(A_p)\, u_{32} \ll 1 .

Tensor Core Jacobian (compile-time option)

Built with make TENSORCORE=1 and selected with JACOBIAN TENSORCORE. A connection residual is a product of eleven features z = (\lambda_\beta, \Delta\Phi_\beta, b_\beta^{\mathrm{up}}, R_s^{\mathrm{up}}, R_v^{\mathrm{up}}), so its Jacobian is the chain rule written as a matrix product,

\frac{\partial R_{ij}}{\partial x_{ij}} = \underbrace{\frac{\partial R_{ij}}{\partial z}}_{L_{\mathrm{flux}}\ \in\ \mathbb{R}^{b_s\times 11}}\ \underbrace{\frac{\partial z}{\partial x_{ij}}}_{L_{\mathrm{props}}\ \in\ \mathbb{R}^{11\times 2b_s}},

padded to 16\times16 tiles and issued for all connections as one batched GEMM in TF32 with FP32 accumulation. The default build compiles none of this.
