Preconditioner ablation: HYPRE and AmgX

NVRS-CUDA on NVIDIA GB200 · generated 2026-09-21 10:53

Only the pressure multigrid differs between the two arms. The outer Krylov is FP64 FGMRES over a df64-emulated FP64 operator, the quasi-IMPES weights that form the pressure system are FP64, and the second CPR stage is the same block-Jacobi correction in both arms. HYPRE and AmgX run their multigrid in FP32. The three Ginkgo arms (FP16, FP32 and FP16 with adaptive-precision block-Jacobi) are compared on Norne only, in compare_NORNE.html: on these cases they were far slower.

Select with LINSOLVER AMGX or LINSOLVER HYPRE. AmgX is the default.

SPE10 model 2 — 60 x 220 x 85 = 1,122,000 cells, 1000 days (decks/SPE10_MATCHED.DATA)

The Tenth SPE Comparative Solution Project, model 2: the geostatistical field with a 107 permeability contrast that is the standard AMG stress test. Real rock rather than a synthetic refinement. Run with the Flow-matched deck and method (SWOF, CPRW, LINTOL 3e-2, RESTART 60, EMULATE 0), AmgX and HYPRE on one GPU. The HYPRE arm uses AMGFAST 0 and AMGREUSE 10: the deck's AMGFAST 1 and AMGREUSE 0 were chosen with AmgX, and for BoomerAMG they gave aggressive coarsening and a weak V-cycle (first 100 days: 49.5 s and 14,532 Krylov against 23.4 s and 5,656).
